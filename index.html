About

I am a Computer Science student at De La Salle University specializing in Human-Computer Interactions, artificial intelligence, and software engineering. As a researcher at the Human-X Interactions Lab, I co-develop augmented reality training systems and explore interactive interface design. I co-authored LasallEats which was presented and published at the IEEE FMLDS 2025.

In addition to software engineering, I specialize in data analysis, applied statistics, and quantitative modeling. My academic experience heavily emphasized data preprocessing and postprocessing, statistical analysis, and data interpretation, spanning both predictive model development and outcome-driven insights. I also conduct thematic analysis for qualitative data, usually extracting structured patterns and insights from textual sources.
